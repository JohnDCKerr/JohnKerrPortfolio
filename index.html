About me

A few bits

I’m John, a video producer, editor, and motion graphics artist. I like finding the thread in a complicated story and making it clear, engaging, and worth watching.

As Executive Producer of Multimedia at Media Matters for America, I led a small team while staying hands-on with editing and animation. I worked with researchers, writers, and leadership to deliver investigative pieces, rapid-response videos, and nationally televised content.

My experience also includes educational editing for National Geographic Learning, graphics for History and Discovery programming, and documentary work. Today, I edit broadcast campaign ads for Kepferle Productions and digital ads for Foundation Blue Media.

Outside of work, you’ll usually find me hiking, camping, mountain biking, or exploring local waterways. I’m especially interested in bringing my experience to outdoor, conservation, science, and documentary storytelling.
