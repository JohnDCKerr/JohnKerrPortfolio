About

I'm a video producer and editor with 15+ years of experience creating narrative-driven content for political campaigns, national advocacy media, and broadcast television. My background spans scripting, storyboarding, editing, motion design, and finishing — end to end.

Most recently I led editorial and motion graphics output at Media Matters for America, running a same-day production schedule, owning a flagship donor sizzle reel, and producing video evidence entered in the Alex Jones defamation trial. I care about strong storytelling, clean editing, and staying curious — whether it's a 30-second turnaround or an 18-minute long-form piece, the goal is always the same: make it honest, compelling, and built to make an impact.
